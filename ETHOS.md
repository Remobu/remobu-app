# REMOBU Platform Ethos & Guardrails
**Version 1.0 — May 2026**
*Grounded in global evidence from LMIC digital agriculture research*

---

## PREAMBLE

REMOBU exists to serve the farmer — not to extract from them, not to impress investors
with hype, and not to add complexity to already difficult lives. Every design decision,
feature addition, and business model choice must be tested against this founding commitment.

These guardrails are binding on all product, technology, and commercial decisions.

---

## PILLAR 1: FARMER-FIRST, ALWAYS

**Guardrails:**
- No feature ships if it requires a smartphone beyond basic WhatsApp access
- Time-to-Mastery (TTM) for an unschooled or elderly producer must be < 30 minutes
- Technology must not consume more than 5% of a farmer's labour hours
- Any feature that creates dependency without measurable income uplift must be redesigned
- UI/UX must support local languages (Sesotho first, then Zulu, Xhosa, Shona)
- Seasonal awareness is mandatory — content must align with crop calendars, not calendar months

---

## PILLAR 2: NON-EXTRACTIVE BY DESIGN

**Guardrails:**
- 3:1 Net Margin Rule — for every M1 a farmer spends, income must increase by at least M3
- Farmer data is owned by the farmer — REMOBU holds a licence for aggregated use only
- B2B data sales require explicit farmer opt-in with revenue share returned to farmer pool
- No dark patterns, no artificial urgency, no subscription traps
- Transparency is a requirement; opacity is a disqualifier

---

## PILLAR 3: RETENTION OVER ACQUISITION

**Guardrails:**
- Monthly Active Farmers (MAF) is the primary KPI — not downloads or sign-ups
- A farmer inactive for 21 days triggers a human check-in — not a marketing blast
- Subsidised access must never mask low retention — free-tier churn = product failure
- Fail-fast experimentation on real farmers is prohibited
- Churn analysis is mandatory monthly with documented reason per churned farmer

---

## PILLAR 4: ECOSYSTEM INTEGRATION, NOT REPLACEMENT

**Guardrails:**
- Advisor network must include existing trusted community figures (extension officers,
  cooperative leaders, agro-dealers) — not only credentialed agronomists
- Marketplace must prioritise local input suppliers before national or international ones
- REMOBU must integrate with national agricultural data systems, not duplicate them
- At least one cooperative or agro-dealer partner required before entering any new market
- Financial services offered through licensed partners only — never built in-house

---

## PILLAR 5: VALUE BUNDLING — THE STACK MUST DELIVER

**Guardrails:**
- Platform must always offer minimum two of four stack elements in any active market:
  advisory | inputs | markets | finance
- New markets not entered until full advisory + marketplace stack is operational
- Bundled offerings must demonstrate clear economic return in plain farmer language
- Pricing reflects real willingness to pay — validated through farmer interviews

---

## PILLAR 6: FROM INFORMATION TO TRANSFORMATION

**Guardrails:**
- Every advisory interaction must be actionable — vague responses are a product failure
- Outcome tracking mandatory — yield, income, input cost measured at least annually
- AI responses must cite locally validated agronomic data where available
- Every recommendation must link directly to a marketplace action where applicable

---

## PILLAR 7: PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE ALIGNMENT

**Guardrails:**
- MOU or data-sharing agreement with Lesotho Ministry of Agriculture within 12 months
- Platform architecture must plug into national farmer registries
- REMOBU will not lobby against public extension services — we complement them
- Where government subsidies exist, REMOBU applies as delivery vehicle, not replacement

---

## PILLAR 8: OPERATIONAL RESILIENCE

**Guardrails:**
- WhatsApp AI response time < 5 seconds under normal load
- Graceful degradation: AI down → route to human; human unavailable → acknowledge + callback
- Critical farmer data backed up daily, RTO < 4 hours
- Infrastructure stress-tested at 10x current load before entering any new market

---

## PILLAR 9: MEASUREMENT & ACCOUNTABILITY

| Metric                  | Target              | Red Line                        |
|-------------------------|---------------------|---------------------------------|
| Monthly Active Farmers  | Growing MoM         | < 60% retention = product review|
| Time-to-Mastery (TTM)   | < 30 min            | > 45 min = redesign             |
| Net Margin Ratio        | > 3:1               | < 2:1 = feature freeze          |
| AI Response Accuracy    | > 85% validated     | < 70% = model retrain           |
| Advisor Response Time   | < 4 hours           | > 24 hours = advisor suspension |
| Platform Uptime         | > 99%               | < 95% = incident review         |

---

## WHAT REMOBU WILL NEVER DO

1. Sell farmer data without explicit opt-in and revenue share
2. Deploy a feature that increases farmer cost without demonstrated 3:1 income return
3. Enter a market without a local trusted intermediary partner
4. Use fail-fast experimentation on real farmers
5. Allow off-platform payments between advisors and farmers
6. Build features that require continuous connectivity to function
7. Prioritise investor optics over farmer outcomes
